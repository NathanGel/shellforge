# M12e Settings — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** executed inline (superpowers:executing-plans), one final review.

**Goal:** step 5 of `2026-10-14-m12-modern-ui-design.md` — the gear's Settings as a grouped form in the System Settings style: sections Core and Services, one row per setting, each service's status and actions in its row; Save and Cancel stay.

**Architecture:** keep Settings' own rows (their inputs, status lines, tests and Keychain flow) and dress them as macOS's grouped `Form` draws: a section is a bold heading over one filled box, its rows divided by inset rules. `Form(.grouped)` itself is not used — it scrolls on its own inside a sheet that already scrolls, and its label/value rows would replace every card's fields.

**Spec:** Shellforge/docs/plans/2026-10-14-m12-modern-ui-design.md, step 5.

## Global Constraints

- Colours only in `Palette.swift` (parity digest moves); sizes from `Metrics`; no literal paddings.
- Nothing is written until Save; a refused Save says why and keeps the panel open — unchanged.
- Package targets macOS 13; the app's deployment target is macOS 12 (Shellforge runs 13+).

## Before and after (the user waived per-step sign-off)

| | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A section | A plain heading over separate bordered cards with gaps | macOS's grouped section: the heading, then one filled box (`Palette.groupFill`, measured against a native grouped `Form`) |
| A card | Its own box, with a rule between its header and its fields | A row in the section's box, no box or inner rule of its own; rows divided by a rule inset past the mark |
| Terminal | A card with a checkbox in its body | A row: “Match the terminal to Shellforge” with a trailing switch, the help line under it |

## Review Focus

1. Dark mode: the box must lift off the sheet as the native one does, not sink.
2. The Mac row's chips and Allow… button, and the Environment row's state, still fit one row header.
3. A section with one row (if Environment is absent, Core still has two) draws no stray divider.
4. Service fields inside a filled box: the field fill (M12d) still reads as a field on the box.
5. The sheet still scrolls with its overflow rule (M12d) above Save.
