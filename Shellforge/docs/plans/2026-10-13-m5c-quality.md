## Global Constraints

- Moves are pure: no behaviour change, the same tests green before and after.
- Tests touch no real home, `~/.ssh`, `UserDefaults.standard`, login shell or network.
- macOS 13; design tokens; AiTerm credit headers kept on ported files; commits by path, ending `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`; never push.

## Review Focus

1. **A cleanup that removes a folder a test still uses.** Expected: only after every test has finished, at process exit.
2. **A tunnel start the daemon refuses** (bad params, unknown alias, helper mismatch). Expected: the sidebar says so; nothing is retried by itself.

---

### Task 1: Snapshots at a fixed 2×
- Every test that reads pixels draws at 2×, whatever screens are attached (M5c's first commit fixed the footer geometry suite).
- One helper per test target: `Snapshot.bitmap(of:)`.
- Test `snapshotsAreTwiceThePointSize`.

### Task 2: Tests leave nothing behind
- `TestScratch.folder(_:)` makes temp folders under one per-run root, removed at exit.
- `TestScratch.defaults()` makes defaults suites, removed at exit.
- The ~30 temp-folder sites and 2 defaults suites move to it.
- Daemon: an autouse fixture kills any fake ssh (by its temp `bin` path) left by a failing test.
- Tests:
  - `aScratchFolderLivesUnderTheRunsRoot`
  - `test_no_fake_ssh_outlives_its_test`

### Task 3: Tunnel errors reach the sidebar
- `DaemonLink`'s `startTunnel`, `stopTunnel` and `holdTunnel` no longer drop errors (`try?`).
- A failure is published as `tunnelError`, and the sidebar shows it as its error banner. A mismatch also sets `helperMismatch`.
- Tests:
  - `aRefusedTunnelStartIsSaid`
  - `aRefusedStopIsSaid`

### Task 4: Structure and conventions
- `SidebarModel` (611 lines) gives its New Review, footer and Jira sections to extension files, as Tunnels already is.
- `RCFiles` moves to `RCFiles.swift`.
- The tunnel sheets' 120 pt alias column becomes one constant.
- `Shellforge/README.md`'s stale lines are fixed.
- No new tests; the suite stays green before and after.

### Task 5: Verify and close
- Build; the package, the daemon and `ShellforgeHostTest` pass.
- The PRD's Code quality and Conventions rows say what was done.
